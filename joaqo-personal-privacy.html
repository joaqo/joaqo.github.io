Joaqo Personal: Privacy Policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

"Joaqo Personal" is a private tool used only by the owner of this site (github.com/joaqo) to read, search and send email and manage calendar events in his own Google accounts, from his own computer. It is not offered to anyone else.

It accesses Gmail and Google Calendar data only for the accounts its owner signs in with, and only when he runs it. Data goes directly between Google and his computer. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising. When he uses AI assistants on his computer, the data he asks them to work with is sent to those assistants' providers to answer his requests. Sign-in tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
